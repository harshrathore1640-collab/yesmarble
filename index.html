Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify if my Makrana marble is 100% original?

Original Makrana marble has a dense interlocking calcite crystal structure which reflects light like sugar granules and does not lose shine or yellow over time. We provide authenticity guarantee with every lot.

Do you provide delivery across India?

Yes! We securely pack and ship our marble slabs and tiles via trusted transport logistics to all major cities across India with complete transit insurance.

What is the price range of Makrana Marble?

Prices vary based on whiteness, absence of veins, thickness, and lot size. Contact us directly on WhatsApp at +91 63672 58832 for live stock pricing.
